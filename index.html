🔊 SonicPay Lab v2

Phone-to-phone text transfer using sound — now powered by ggwave (real multi-tone FSK + error correction). Prototype only.

⚠️ Must be served over https:// (or localhost) for microphone access.

📤 SEND

"Near-inaudible" uses ~19-20kHz tones. Most adults won't consciously notice it, but phone speakers/mics are weaker up there, so expect shorter range and more retries than audible mode.
